Bulk import recipes

Upload a CSV with one row per recipe. Ingredient names must already exist in the Ingredients list — anything that doesn't match gets skipped with a reason, so you can add the missing ingredient and re-import just that row.

The instructions cell holds one step per line. The ingredients cell holds one ingredient per line, formatted quantity | ingredient name | unit — e.g.:
